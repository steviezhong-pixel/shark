Build your China supply chain — from first research to shipped orders

We help overseas companies develop and run China supply chains: supplier research and vetting, quote comparison at your quantity, coordinated factory audits, sample-round management, order milestones and pre-shipment inspection — most of it remote from day one, with an on-the-ground team in Guangzhou-Foshan and Shanghai when you visit.

Start a project How it works

  • Remote & day-one: shortlists, evidence notes and priced comparisons while your project is still on a spreadsheet.
  • Gate-keeping: samples, audits and milestones coordinated — invoices and contracts stay between you and the supplier.
  • Honest edges: we do not certify quality, take ownership of goods, or guarantee orders. That is documented on our disclosure page.
  • On the ground in Guangzhou-Foshan & Shanghai: accompanied factory and showroom days, interpreters by category.
  • Reaching you where you start: many buyers find us through trade fair weeks — see the fairs we support — then stay for the supply-chain work.
  • Two engagement modes: remote programs (research, coordination, inspection-driving) and China visit days.

What you can check, without trusting us

  • One scheduled call before any payment — ask anything, bring your itinerary.
  • Sample files: our trip plan, supplier comparison sheet, factory-visit checklist and meeting-notes format.
  • Pricing structure: what is a fixed fee, what is pass-through cost, what you can cancel.
  • Commercial disclosure: who pays us, when, and how recommendations stay independent.
  • We are an independent service provider — not a fair organizer, not a ticket reseller.
