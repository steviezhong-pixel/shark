Who should go

The strongest furniture-industry cluster week in Guangzhou, with a natural extension to Foshan showroom sourcing. Furniture importers & retail chains, designers & fit-out contractors, hotel/apartment project buyers, office-furniture dealers, component buyers.

Suggested arrival

Book hotels in Pazhou or along metro access to both venues. Arrive at least 1 day before your phase start. Pre-register online — registration at the door is slower.

Supplier districts worth combining with the fair

Foshan Lecong furniture market (30–70 min drive), Shunde case-goods factories, Zhongshan lighting (60–90 min).

What goChina Events does around this show

We combine show days with Foshan (Lecong) showroom days and factory visits for your short-list.

Questions buyers actually ask

  • Is CIFF Guangzhou worth visiting for a first-time furniture buyer?
    If your list spans furniture categories or you also buy decor/lighting, yes: one fair day covers more suppliers than a week of showroom visits. If you only need one known product family, a direct Foshan factory run may be more efficient.
  • Do I need to attend both stages (both date blocks)?
    Home furniture buyers rarely need the office/materials block; project buyers (office, hotel) more often do. We plan your trip around your product list, not the full fair.
  • When should supplier visits be scheduled?
    Between the two date blocks or after your last fair day — factory days collide with fair-walking otherwise.
