Meeting Notes Template

Sample of our same-evening meeting notes. Delivered after each accompanied meeting day when included in your package.

  • Present: names, roles, contact level (owner / sales / technical).
  • Confirmed by both sides: specifications & quantities discussed, prices and their assumptions, conditions in writing.
  • Checked: samples, documents, certifications presented and recorded.
  • Open items: every question pending, with the owner & promised date.
  • Next steps: what each side will do next, by which date.
