Pricing & fees

One price list can't be honest across industries and cities, so every engagement runs on an itemized written quote. What is always true:

TypeHow it works
Fixed coordination feePer trip/package, scoped on the planning call. Stated once in the quote.
Day ratesInterpreters and vehicles by day length, city and technical level; overtime stated upfront.
Per-piece workSupplier research & shortlists by category and list depth.
Pass-through at actualHotels, transport, meals, third-party inspection, freight — separate lines at real provider rates, never silently marked up.

Three budget examples (structure, not committed prices)

Supply-chain kickoff (remote)

Companies starting or expanding a China supply chain — does not require a trip.

Service components:

  • Supplier research & structured screening
  • Evidence-noted shortlist with per-supplier open questions
  • Quote collection at your quantity (optional add)
  • Comparison-ready deliverable you can forward internally

Pass-through (at actual cost):

  • Third-party audit / inspection fees, if commissioned, at actual provider rates

Cooperation income: No supplier-side income in this format.

Not included: Quality certification · Order guarantees · Travel coordination (addable later)

Amounts: published on the itemized quote once scope is agreed — current list prices not yet public.

Show-escort day

Buyers already attending a fair who want one organized day of accompanied visit & interpreting.

Service components:

  • Coordination & pre-trip planning
  • Business interpreter (day rate)
  • Business vehicle (day rate)
  • Meeting notes same evening

Pass-through (at actual cost):

  • Hotel, meals
  • Fair entry cost if any (you pay the organizer)
  • Parking/tolls

Cooperation income: No supplier-side income in this format.

Not included: Supplier shortlist research (bookable separately) · Factory scheduling

Amounts: published on the itemized quote once scope is agreed — current list prices not yet public.

3-day trade show + supplier visits

Buyers anchoring one fair and extending with supplier/showroom days around it.

Service components:

  • Trip plan & hotel options
  • Supplier shortlist (5–8 candidates) built pre-trip
  • Accompanied visit days & interpreting
  • Comparison sheet + 2 follow-up rounds

Pass-through (at actual cost):

  • Hotels, transport, meals
  • Third-party costs at actual rates (done after your confirmation)

Cooperation income: Where supplier-side income exists, disclosed above — never folded invisibly into a supplier we recommend.

Not included: Quality certification · Order guarantees

Amounts: published on the itemized quote once scope is agreed — current list prices not yet public.

5–7 day full sourcing trip

Buyers building a multi-supplier buying program across districts (furniture + lighting; machine + packaging; textile + sampling...).

Service components:

  • Full requirement brief & route design
  • Supplier shortlist, reviews & appointments per specialty
  • Accompanied days with records
  • Post-trip coordination window (agreed)

Pass-through (at actual cost):

  • Hotels, intercity transport
  • Third parties (inspectors, forwarders) at actual rates

Cooperation income: Supplier-side income by agreement, disclosed in the quote before you confirm; you keep multi-candidate selection.

Not included: We never own your goods · No importer-of-record role

Amounts: published on the itemized quote once scope is agreed — current list prices not yet public.
