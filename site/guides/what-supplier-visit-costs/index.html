What a supplier visit day really costs

Cost components for a visit day (vehicle, interpreter, time, distance pitfalls) so quotes can be compared honestly.

When buyers ask what a supplier visit costs, they usually expect a single number. The honest answer has parts — and the parts differ by city, distance and what you actually need.

Cost components of a typical visit day

ComponentWhat drives the price
Business vehicle + driverVehicle class, day length, distance. Cross-district days (e.g. Guangzhou to Lecong and back) cost more than city-internal days.
Business interpreterLanguage pair, technical depth, overtime beyond the agreed hours.
Scheduling work (before the trip)List size: confirming 5 factories that will actually host you takes more work than booking a taxi.
Records & follow-upSame-day written notes and structured comparison reports take real hours; cheap quotes usually mean they are skipped.
Pass-through costsMeals, parking, tolls, entrance tickets where actual costs apply — usually billed at cost, not marked up invisibly.

Why quotes look so different

Two quotes for "a visit day" can differ by multiples because one includes scheduling, interpreting and records, and the other is a driver with a phone. Neither is wrong — but compare them component by component.

Where costs hide

  • Waiting time between visits — agree whether it is in the day fee or billed separately.
  • Overtime — meetings run long; the rate should be stated before you book.
  • Long routes — a route with a 90-minute drive between stops may need an extra vehicle day or a different base city.
