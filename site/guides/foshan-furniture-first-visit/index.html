Buying furniture in Foshan: how to prepare your first visit

What a useful Foshan furniture day looks like: product list, MOQs, showroom vs factory, transport and records.

Foshan (especially the Lecong furniture district) is where many overseas furniture buyers start. Showrooms there are dense, suppliers multiply fast, and first-time visitors often end up with a lot of photos and no usable comparison. What makes a Foshan day useful is preparation, not mileage.

Before the trip: build a product list, not a wish list

  • Item list with quantities (e.g. 300 dining chairs, 40 hotel headboards).
  • Materials and finish expectations (fabric grade, foam density, veneer vs solid wood).
  • Target price band per item — a range is fine, but state it.
  • Delivery date and your country's compliance needs (e.g. flame-retardancy rules for contract furniture).

Suppliers in Lecong range from showroom-only resellers to factories with their own workshops. Both can be valid answers — but knowing which you are talking to changes what you should verify.

A realistic visit day

Three or four focused stops is a full day. Lecong showrooms are kilometers apart, traffic is real, and every showroom visit consumes 40–90 minutes if you actually discuss specifications. A route that "sees ten places" usually sees them the way a tourist sees a museum.

What to actually record

  • Price basis: what quantity and options that price assumes.
  • MOQ per item and mixing rules for small orders.
  • Sample policy: sample cost, lead time, and whether it is credited on bulk orders.
  • Who is responsible for quality claims after shipment — and in writing.

After the visit

The value of the trip appears later: quotes arrive in different formats, at different speeds. A structured comparison sheet (same fields for every supplier: spec, price basis, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, open questions) is what turns a trip into a decision. Notice: this guide is general advice built from how the market works, not a claim about any specific supplier's current situation. Verify everything against your actual quotes.
